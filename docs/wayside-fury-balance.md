## COMBAT — Chapter 1 and Space challenge pass

This pass supersedes the fixed combat tuning above. Chapter routing, ability gates, first-clear reward allowlists and Update 1 hooks retain their existing contracts.

- **Boss pressure:** Sentinel, Watcher, Cheese Inspector and Warden share six-hit poise and an immediate wall-pressure escape. A 0.65-second ground-circle warning precedes the burst; 0.95 seconds of damage immunity covers the warning and escape. Bosses ignore ordinary hit-stun, take reduced knockback, and sweep up to 96 units along a clear inward ray. Static collision remains authoritative. The existing 50% phase transitions now start protected bursts; Warden's pylon shield and low-HP pattern change remain intact. Recovery lasts 1.4 seconds.
- **Scaling:** encounters use an authored minimum and the party's rounded average combat level. Remote contributions are bounded to the host's level ±2; guests use the host encounter band ±2. Rescaling retains enemy HP percentages and existing one-to-four-player multipliers. Chapter 1 HP grows with derived power; lunar HP uses level-derived power rather than the largest remote gear value. Main-route XP still reaches level 6; both caches reach level 7. Content more than three levels below the personal sheet yields diminishing XP, `base / (1 + 0.5 × excess levels)`. Co-op kill receipts carry their source level and each seat applies diminishing XP once against its permanent sheet, including delayed rewards after a map change. Legacy receipts without the optional source level keep their original XP. The shared XP hook caps boosts at 1.5×. No XP booster inventory was added.
- **Melee and dodge:** tapping retains the three-hit string. Holding the combat Attack action for 0.6 seconds, then releasing, adds a 2.8× strike with 48-unit reach and 180 knockback, costing 18 stamina. Holds can start during combo recovery. Talk/use presses never arm a charge; pause, dash, guard, Ki, dialogue and swap cancel it. Dash retains 0.18-second motion and 0.23-second invulnerability. Touch, J and gamepad A use the same input path.
- **Crew identities:** Joe's combo finisher has stronger shield-breaking force and his concentrated Wayside Wave remains. Matt retains the three-ray Golden Fury and restores 25 stamina on signature release. Alex retains Twin Comet and gains 0.45-second evasive protection. Jon's broad Night Breaker restores 12% maximum HP to living members of his local tag party; it never revives a KO. You retains Fury Wave.
- **Chapter 1 archetypes:** zombies alternate a marked charging lane and orbiting swarm flank; imps kite and telegraph shots; pumpkins brace against frontal light hits and commit to a bash; ghosts circle before a telegraphed ambush. Finishers/charged strikes bypass pumpkin bracing. Space retains its seven distinct behaviors. No new recolor enemies were added.
- **Hard:** an optional Settings choice at any time, with a Chapter 1 completion message. Incoming damage is 1.45×, cooldowns advance 1.3×, kiters strafe, chargers flank on approach, and bosses/lunar attackers lead moving local targets. Telegraph durations are retained. Co-op uses host difficulty, carries charge poses, and restores each player's personal preference on leaving. Settings changes are disabled during co-op.
- **Compatibility:** save v1/v2/v3/v4 each explicitly sanitize the additive preference to Normal when absent or invalid; explicit Hard persists. The format stays v4 because no durable encounter fields were added. Existing HOME/checkpoint and receipt migrations remain. Bounded optional combat snapshot fields preserve legacy envelopes and existing new-area protocol fencing. Difficulty does not award tickets.

Regression coverage lives in `scripts/check-wayside-fury-combat.mjs` and is included by the balance check. It exercises all four boss types at every arena edge with four surrounding players, true burst immunity, collision-safe escape, phases, shields, five archetypes, charged release, signatures, dodge, XP caps, high-level revisits, ratio-preserving co-op scaling, Hard damage, save defaults and protocol validation. `scripts/check-wayside-fury-combat-browser.mjs` exercises keyboard, touch and gamepad holds/releases, Settings, pause cancellation, safe-context handling, native backing and repeated-draw immutability in default 2D and optional 3D; every launch uses `--mute-audio`.

The Normal default-stat simulation clears all ten Blast zones and the realm with 68 kills, level 7, 307 candy and no deaths; Sentinel is about 27 seconds and Watcher 21 seconds. These are deterministic bot measurements. Real-device thermal/FPS and human difficulty assessment remain unmeasured.

Hard also clears all ten Blast zones and the realm without deaths using starter gear, a You/Jon tag party, charged strikes, combo taps and telegraph-driven dodges. It reaches level 7 with 68 kills and 313 candy (including the existing six-candy glovebox find). This tactical bot is separate from Normal’s baseline; its timings are not a human difficulty target.
