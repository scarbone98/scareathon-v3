# Overworld visual audit — October 8, 2026

Baseline: `origin/main` at `bb026c3e`. Original chapter plan and design review were read first. Scope is the county overworld only; room interiors, Blast-room ground, combat, saves, rewards, progression and Update 1 are outside this change.

The matrix covers 16 locations in default Canvas 2D and optional real Metal WebGL 3D at 390 × 844 DPR 3 and 1440 × 900 DPR 2. Before and after captures use paused, native-DPR inspection fixtures at the same coordinates/time. They establish visual detail and layering, not sustained real-phone FPS or thermal performance. Some fixture coordinates deliberately put the taxi over water to expose the bank; they do not represent player reachability or a floating-taxi bug. Guest avatar appearances can differ between contexts.

The county has no authored elevated road overpass or zebra crosswalk. The reservoir causeway is its water crossing; junction stop bars and entrance thresholds are included in the road audit. Dungeon bridges belong to the separate room-ground pass and were not modified. No extra crossings were invented for screenshots.

The reservoir's accidental dry strips now become water collision, while authored causeway and loop routes remain open. All-map radius-aware reachability, clear road centerlines, exits, original finds and co-op checks verify this correction. Minimap materials use the terrain palette and the same rounded water contours before drawing its roads and markers.

## Verification

Passed: isolated `origin/main` TypeScript + production build; `npx tsc -b` after the water/road integration; ESLint on changed source and capture files; `npm run check:wayside-fury`; collision, areas/reachability, roads, road-network, road-clearance, minimap, grounding, overworld-audit and audit-paint scripts. All 81 authored exits remain reachable. No server files changed.

Both matrices contain 64 screenshots and 64 native-DPR metric records. All captures launch with `--mute-audio`, assert the visible canvas backing size and report no uncaught browser errors. [Before metrics](before/metrics.json), [After metrics](after/metrics.json).

The final crater-bank TypeScript rerun and the full real-WebGL 3D browser check are still running; their final results will be recorded before completion.

Reproduce on a Vite server at port 5232 with `FURY_BASE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:5232 node scripts/capture-wayside-fury-audit-matrix.mjs after`. Set `FURY_AUDIT_RECAPTURE=1` to replace captures, or leave it unset to resume interrupted captures and missing metrics. `FURY_CAPTURE_SIZE=390|1440`, `FURY_CAPTURE_GFX=2d|3d` and `FURY_CAPTURE_SPOTS=name,...` select a subset; `FURY_PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` selects the installed Playwright module. Screenshots are original game rendering, with no borrowed art or image upscaling.
