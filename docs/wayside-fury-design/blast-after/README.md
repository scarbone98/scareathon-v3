# BLAST Site rebuild

Ten original Chapter 1 room IDs and names, dimensions, exit IDs/targets/arrival coordinates, clear gates, mini-boss/boss indices and encounter budgets are preserved. Each room now has its own authored terrain and landmark arrangement in `game/blastLayouts.ts`. New original artwork in `game/blastArt.ts` is cached at four source pixels per world unit and drawn with native Canvas curves and material detail. `?gfx=3d` uses the same production Canvas dungeon path, collision and simulation.

Scorched Road loops around a burned convoy and asphalt impacts. Split Creek has a continuous channel, banks, an unusable broken crossing and two usable crossings. Ruined Yard circles a collapsed farmhouse and tractor through staggered fence gaps. Furnace Pass has canyon shoulders, ember vents and timber catwalk loops. Sentinel Gate leaves a broad central combat lane between fortified walls and four cover pillars. Shattered Courtyard loops around a toppled statue and ruined fountain. Rift Approach has cracked ground and levitating debris. Watcher's Hollow keeps a large pale, readable fighting bowl framed by solid vent rims and shards. Ash Orchard has dead rows, hay bales, a scout and its original cache. Old Supply Depot has separate shelving aisles, a forklift and loading dock.

Existing personal collectible IDs, coordinates and rewards are retained. Two additive, lore-only Chapter 1 finds fill the previously chapter-gated Rift/Hollow rooms; they award no currency or tickets. All rooms expose a stable `radarAnchors` secret location for future integration. No Update 1 feature, campaign registry, save format or protocol change is introduced. Existing save sanitization already supports additive known collectible IDs; no save-version migration is required by this content-only update. Existing saves resume safely in town. Cache IDs remain `loot-blast-8` and `loot-blast-9`.

`before-0.png` through `before-9.png` and matching `after-*.png` are 1440 × 900 DPR 2 whole-room inspection fixtures, drawn using the production terrain, props and actor methods. These deliberately show the entire creek and canyon rather than only the opening camera view. They are native-detail art evidence, not FPS or thermal measurements. Metrics record prop/encounter counts and backing dimensions. Rendering is checked for simulation immutability.

Reproduce with Vite on port 5216 and `node scripts/capture-wayside-fury-blast.mjs before|after`. Set `FURY_PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` to a Playwright `index.mjs` path and optionally `PLAYWRIGHT_EXECUTABLE_PATH` to an installed Chromium executable. Every launch includes `--mute-audio`. Before fixtures require the original `world.ts` from the parent of rebuild commit `fb89f81`.

The collision script path-finds with swept, radius-aware edges from spawn to every exit, authored encounter, simulated enemy and existing/new collectible. It also checks safe destination arrivals, cache/scout interactions, original encounter budgets and landmark coverage. The chapter plan's section 11 is unchanged.

Verification: `npx tsc -b`; ESLint on all changed source/scripts; `npm run check:wayside-fury`; collision, collectibles, campaign, co-op simulation/rewards, combat and balance scripts. The final starter-stat route clears every room and the realm with 68 kills, 307 candy and no deaths. Boss edge/phase checks and exact ticket-delta checks pass. The server suite initially hit an unrelated Monster Bash 5-second timeout on the loaded host; `cd server && npx jest --forceExit --testTimeout=60000` passes all 40 suites / 463 tests. All ten final browser parity comparisons pass at 1440 × 900 DPR 2. No sustained real-device performance claim is made.
